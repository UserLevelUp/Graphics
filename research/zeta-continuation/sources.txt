CONTINUATION PRESENTATION — SOURCES AND PRECISE REUSE
Sources checked: 2026-11-15

Elias Wegert, Phase Plots of Complex Functions: a Journey in Illustration
https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2295
Phase-coloring foundation. Our four-quadrant palette is a quantization choice, not a claimed reproduction of Wegert's palette.

Stephen J. Lester, a-Points of the Riemann zeta-function on the critical line
https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.0169
Established research on nonzero zeta a-points. We do not implement its distribution theorems.

Platt and Trudgian, The Riemann hypothesis is true up to 3·10^12
https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09765
Rigorous interval verification through height 3·10^12. Our height-10-to-30 animation adds no new verified RH height.

Lamzouri, A new proof that more than 2/3 of the zeros are simple and on the critical line
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02882v2
September 2026 preprint states >67.25% simple and on line. It describes earlier Claude/Anthropic work verified by Alpöge and Furman. We have not independently checked these proofs; this is not a journal-acceptance or percent-RH-solved claim.

OpenAI math repository, The quasi-Riemann hypothesis: formalization scope
https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/003.md
OpenAI reports a zero-free half-plane Re(s)>7/8 and Lean formalization. We did not replay that formalization or verify its new proof. It is not an assumption in this experiment.

NIST DLMF 25.10(i), Riemann zeta zeros
https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10.i
RH concerns all nontrivial zeta zeros and the input critical line.

mpmath zeta-function documentation
https://mpmath.org/doc/current/functions/zeta.html
Zeta values, derivatives, Euler–Maclaurin evaluation and zero seeds; reference environment pins mpmath 1.3.0.
