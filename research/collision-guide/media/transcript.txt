00:09:03,750  Where the connection to RH actually lies
The Riemann entire function has a heat deformation called H sub t, with the same heat sign. Its zeros are all real exactly when t is at least a threshold, Lambda. RH is equivalent to Lambda being at most zero. Rodgers and Tao proved Lambda is at least zero. Thus RH is equivalent to Lambda equaling zero. Shared zero dynamics connect the questions.

00:09:33,750  The missing step is control of the infinite system
Our certificate uses finitely many roots and a finite diameter. The Riemann flow has infinitely many zeros, so an extension needs rigorous control of distant zeros and convergence of their interactions. Assuming every zero is real at time zero would assume RH. A uniform real-root interval below zero would contradict the known lower bound on Lambda. The open task is control toward zero from a known-real regime.
