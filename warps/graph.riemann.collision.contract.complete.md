Baseline: commit 170993c on graph/riemann-local-origins. The earlier quadratic color laboratory demonstrated an exact two-zero collision and a frozen quadratic forecast that is exact for that quadratic. It did not establish that the forecast controls interacting zeros of higher-degree functions.

The bounded obligation is to classify all real parameter values for which every zero of

P_tau(z) = z^4 - (10 + 12*tau) z^2 + 9 + 20*tau + 12*tau^2

is real; compare the exact first collision with frozen local quadratic and isolated-gap forecasts; and record explicit counterexamples with exact rational arithmetic. This family satisfies partial_tau P = -partial_z^2 P.

Acceptance requires a human algebraic proof covering every real tau, an independently implemented exact identity checker, negative controls that reject corrupted inputs, and an explicit statement of the scope of every counterexample. A numerical plot is explanatory evidence only. Freeze the evidence with content hashes and a local Git restore point.

The parent RH obligation cannot close on this result. A completed child means this polynomial control and the specified forecast audit are resolved. Recur's declared evidence gates record review and artifact identity; they are not a proof-assistant kernel and do not establish mathematical truth themselves. No novelty or RH proof is claimed. Recur Lang adds no required validation to this algebraic task and is not used as a proof checker.

The actual Riemann deformation H_t and its de Bruijn-Newman threshold Lambda are different objects. RH is equivalent to Lambda <= 0, while Rodgers and Tao proved Lambda >= 0. Those facts motivate studying collisions, but transferring an inequality from this quartic to H_t would require a separate argument. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.05914 .
